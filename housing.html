§ — The constraint nobody tells you about

Your problem isn't price. It's that you only need 74 nights.

Standard French leases run nine to twelve months. You need 5 October to 18 December — two and a half months, starting after the academic year's housing has already been allocated in June and July. Most residences will simply refuse the dates before anyone discusses money. That is the filter to solve first.

The lease type that solves it

Ask for a « bail mobilité » by name. It's a French lease category built for exactly your situation: one to ten months, furnished, for students and people on temporary assignment. Two things make it decisive — the landlord cannot legally demand a security deposit on one, and Visale covers it.

Most listings won't advertise it. Ask anyway. A landlord facing an empty flat from October will often agree to a mobility lease when a nine-month contract was never going to happen.

§ — The guarantor problem

Visale is free. Never pay for a guarantor.

Almost every French landlord wants a garant — someone French, salaried and liable for your rent. You don't have one. The state solved this and it costs nothing.

  • Visale covers you. Free, state-backed, open to international students aged 18–30 regardless of income. You qualify: you have a long-stay student visa (VLS-TS « étudiant »), which is the condition for non-EU applicants.
  • Apply now, not when you find a flat. The eligibility certificate takes 2 to 15 days, and you must hold it before signing. Turning up to a viewing without one is how people lose flats to the next applicant. Apply at visale.fr.
  • The 2026 reform raised the ceilings — roughly €840/month in larger cities and €680 elsewhere. Grenoble student rents sit comfortably under either, so this won't bite.
  • Studapart's own guarantee is the alternative if a landlord won't take Visale. It's bundled into their platform and paid, so treat it as the fallback rather than the default.
Don't bother with CAF housing benefit. Standard advice for exchange students, and wrong for you: APL applications take two to three months to process and your entire lease is two and a half. You would complete the paperwork after flying home. Skip it and spend the hours on the Iceland car instead.

§ — Where in the city

Live near the station, not near the nightlife

A normal exchange student should optimise for bars. You should optimise for Grenoble Gare — because eleven weekend windows plus day trips means something like twenty departures and twenty returns, most of them on a Friday evening when you're tired and it's dark.
