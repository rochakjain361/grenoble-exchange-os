§ — 7 – 15 November

Nine days away for three missed classes

Spain and Portugal cannot be done in a weekend, and the reason is structural: Madrid to Lisbon has no usable rail link. The direct night train was discontinued and what survives is ten hours with changes. Add seven hours to reach Barcelona and Iberia needs six days minimum. So it has to come out of teaching time — and one week costs dramatically less than the rest.

Why this week, and no other

Three separate accidents line up. Armistice Day falls on Wednesday 11 November — a public holiday in the exact middle of the week, welding two weekends into one block. The Eco-Design intensive is already finished (it runs 14–28 October, five sessions total, and missing two of those would cost 40% of a course). And Marketing of Innovation has only just started on 5 November, so you miss its second week — the least damaging point in any course.

Toussaint week is the obvious alternative and it is worse: the same nine days, but 1 November falls on a Sunday in 2026 so the holiday is wasted, Eco-Design is still running, and you'd miss five teaching days instead of three.

Check three things before booking. The course dates above are last year's — reliable for structure, not for your 2026 calendar, so confirm no intensive runs 7–15 November. Check the attendance cap per course: French business schools commonly allow one or two absences and exceeding it can fail you, a grade that transfers back to IIMB. And tell professors in September, not November — advance notice gets you materials and moved deadlines; retroactive excuses get you nothing.
