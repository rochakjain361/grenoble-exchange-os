§ — Phase 3 · 5 October – 18 December

The finding this whole plan turns on

Reading the GEM timetable for your six courses gives one fact worth more than any other in this document: Monday is free about 80% of the time. Saturday and Sunday always are. Tuesday, Wednesday and Friday are locked at 90%.

So the travel unit is not a weekend — it is Friday evening to Monday night, eleven times over. Most exchange students never work this out and take Saturday to Sunday all semester. That free Monday is the difference between Annecy again and Rome.
