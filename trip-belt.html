§ — Phase 1 · 11 – 24 September

Seven countries, and the pass pays for almost none of it

This route exists because of one fact: in Germany, Austria, Slovakia, Hungary, Czechia and Poland, intercity trains carry a rail pass with no reservation and no fee. You board and sit down. That is why the fortnight goes east rather than north — the same 14 days in Scandinavia would cost roughly three times as much and reach three countries instead of seven.

Routed around Oktoberfest. The 2026 festival runs 19 September to 4 October and Munich beds go three to four times normal from the 19th. This itinerary has you in Munich on the 13th–15th and out of Bavaria before the surge, moving east into progressively cheaper countries as the fortnight goes on.

The one thing to get right

You cannot do this carrying 35 kg. One 23 kg case goes into storage in Paris on day one and a ~12 kg backpack travels. You collect the case on 3 October when the Iceland flight lands back at CDG — which is precisely why Paris is the right storage city and Grenoble is the wrong one.
