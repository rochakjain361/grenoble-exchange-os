The efficient frontier

Twelve countries are already locked in. These seven take you to nineteen for about three extra days and zero extra missed classes — because each one hangs off a journey you are making anyway. Slovenia and Croatia ride on Venice; Luxembourg on the Strasbourg Christmas markets; Liechtenstein on your Swiss weekend; Monaco is twenty minutes from Nice.

Past nineteen, the economics invert. Every further country needs its own dedicated block — the Baltics, the Balkans, Scandinavia — costing days and classes rather than hours. Nineteen is where the cheap countries run out.
