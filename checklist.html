§ — First fortnight in Grenoble

October is admin month, and one item is load-bearing

The OFII validation is the one that quietly gates the rest of your semester — because without it, the Balkans trip becomes a border problem rather than a holiday.

blockerValidate your visa with OFIIDo this in your first fortnight in Grenoble. Your VLS-TS only functions as a residence permit once validated — and the Balkans trip takes you out of Schengen and back in. Re-entry on an unvalidated visa is where people get stopped. What we're skipping, and why that's the right call

SkippedReason

Strasbourg Christmas marketsAbsorbed by the Balkans block. The genuine loss in that trade.

And the three genuine unknowns

1 · Exam dates. Everything assumes term ends 18 December. If an exam sits after that, the December end of the plan moves.
2 · The real 2026 timetable. The free-Monday finding, the Eco-Design intensive, the Armistice week — all inferred from last year's pattern for the same six courses. Reliable for structure, not for dates.
3 · Who else is coming. Iceland at four sharing is €1,000 a head; alone it's €1,850. The Grenoble flat at three sharing is €453/month; alone it's €1,359. The two largest lines in your entire budget are both decided by how many people you find, and neither is decided yet.
