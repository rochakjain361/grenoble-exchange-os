§ — Phase 2 · 25 September – 3 October

The most expensive country on the itinerary, and the hardest to eat in

Nine days is the shortest trip where driving the whole Ring Road makes sense — under seven, don't attempt it. Two things decide what this costs: how many people split the car, and how much food you carried from India.

Do this before you book anything else

Find three other people. Car, fuel and a guesthouse room all divide by four. Alone, nine days in Iceland is €1,800+; split four ways it is €950–1,100. The GEM exchange cohort and the Indian student groups are full of people looking at the same window — ask in early September, not October.

Then book the car, before the flights. Icelandic rental stock is small and September is still shoulder-season demand. Beds you can find late; a 4×4 you cannot.

This is where the food you carried earns its weight. A kilo of ready-to-eat carried into Iceland returns roughly €83 against buying meals — about eight times what the same kilo returns during term in Grenoble, where you have a kitchen and an Indian grocer. Two packs a day for nine days is about 5.4 kg, and it is the single best-justified weight in your luggage. A campervan compounds this: it folds bed and transport into one line and gives you somewhere to cook.
